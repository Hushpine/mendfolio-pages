Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Mendfolio is a local-first photo reporting app from Hushpine. Your project library is stored on your device; optional iCloud features use Apple’s services, not a Hushpine project server.

This policy explains the iOS app’s handling of information, including photos, project records, purchases, and files you choose to share.

1. Information you add and what it is used for

  • Project content: project names, addresses and other details you enter, rooms, issues, notes, open/done status, before/after photos, annotations, ordering, and revision history. The app uses this to organize your work and create reports and backups.
  • Business details: the business name and contact details you save for use on newly generated PDFs. A previously exported PDF remains a separate snapshot; changing your settings does not update it.
  • Local operational records: record identifiers, timestamps, file references and integrity hashes, settings, purchase entitlement cache, and synchronization queue/status. These support saving, recovery, purchase access, and optional synchronization.

No Mendfolio account is required. Hushpine does not operate a backend that receives your project library. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites in the app. We do not sell your project information or use it for advertising.

2. Camera, photos, and permissions

The app requests camera or photo-library access when needed to take or select project photos. You can decline or change permissions in iOS Settings. Declining access does not stop you from opening existing projects, but may prevent the corresponding photo operation. The app requests no location permission, microphone permission, contacts permission, or advertising tracking permission.

Original photos may contain EXIF, GPS, camera, and other embedded metadata from the source file even though Mendfolio does not request your location. The app keeps an imported original and creates derived images for display and reporting. Default photo sharing uses derived images rather than copying the original file’s EXIF container; this is not a guarantee that all possible identifying information is removed. Faces, addresses, labels, and location clues visible in the photo remain visible.

Choosing original-photo ZIP sharing or a full project ZIP backup includes originals, which may retain metadata. Review the contents before sharing.

3. Optional iCloud synchronization and recovery

When iCloud is configured and available in your build and you enable it in Settings & backup, the app sends project records, revision snapshots, and associated original and derived photos to your Apple account’s CloudKit private database. Apple processes and stores that data to support synchronization and recovery using the same Apple account. A hashed iCloud account identifier is stored locally to help prevent an existing library from silently uploading to a different account.

iCloud requires an eligible signed-in Apple account, available storage, network access, and the relevant Apple services. Some builds may not have iCloud configured. Check the app’s sync status and last-success information; enabling the switch does not prove an upload has completed. iOS controls background execution, so uploads and recovery may be delayed, interrupted, or unavailable. We do not guarantee a successful automatic backup or uninterrupted synchronization.

You can turn off iCloud synchronization in Settings & backup to stop further sync work. Turning it off does not delete already uploaded records or local data. Deletion state may synchronize, while older snapshots and photo assets remain retained. Synchronization is not a substitute for an independent backup.

Apple’s handling of its services is described in the Apple Privacy Policy and applicable iCloud terms. We make no separate promise of end-to-end encryption for this app’s CloudKit data.

4. Purchases and Apple services

Optional purchases and restoration use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. The app processes product and transaction information and a local entitlement cache to verify access to the project upgrade. Apple handles the payment method, billing, and refund process; Hushpine does not receive your payment card details. Purchase queries, buying, restoring, and entitlement checks may require a network connection. Restoring a purchase does not restore project files.

Apple may separately process service, diagnostic, and purchase information under its own policies and your Apple settings. The absence of an app analytics SDK does not mean Apple’s operating system or App Store never processes information.

5. Reports, sharing, and independent ZIP backups

PDFs and photo ZIPs can include project or client information, business contact details, photos, annotations, and issue status. Full project ZIP backups include project records, annotations, and associated original and derived photos. ZIP files created by the app are not encrypted.

Export files are created on the device. You choose whether and where to send or save them through system sharing or file controls. The recipient, cloud drive, email provider, or other service you choose then handles the copy under its own terms. Hushpine does not control those recipients or external copies, and later changes or deletions in Mendfolio do not update or recall them.

6. Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • Local library: projects and files remain in app storage until removed with an available control or the app’s local data is removed. Archiving only changes visibility. Removing a photo from an issue does not guarantee its original file has been erased.
  • Recently Deleted and revision history: deleting a project moves it to Recently Deleted. This is a recoverable deletion state, not immediate permanent erasure. Revision snapshots, deletion records, and original assets are not automatically purged on a fixed schedule in the current version.
  • Generated exports: exported PDFs and ZIPs may remain in app storage; the current version does not automatically clean all generated export files. Copies saved or shared outside the app must be removed separately by you or their recipients.
  • Removing the app: an uninstall normally removes its local sandbox. Offloading the app is different and can retain data. Neither action guarantees removal of iCloud records, device backups, or externally saved files. Reinstalling and re-enabling sync may restore retained cloud records.
  • iCloud copies: use Apple’s iCloud account/storage controls, where available, to manage cloud data. The app currently has no permanent cloud-purge control; deleting a project or disabling sync does not erase all historical cloud copies. Contact support if you need help identifying the available controls before relying on deletion.

You can edit information in the app, revoke camera/photo permissions in iOS Settings, disable optional sync, and manage copies you exported. iOS device backups depend on your Apple/device settings and are separate from an independent project ZIP. Keep a tested backup outside the app before uninstalling or changing devices. We cannot promise recovery from an incomplete sync or a lost local library.

7. Support correspondence and privacy requests

If you email Hushpine, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you send through our email provider. We use them to respond and address the issue, not for advertising. We retain support correspondence while needed for the request and legitimate recordkeeping or legal obligations; you can request deletion, correction, or access by contacting us. We will address applicable requests and explain any lawful retention or technical limits. We cannot recall copies held by other recipients or promise to erase private iCloud data through support email.

8. Security, children, and this website

Device access controls and Apple’s platform protections help protect app data, but no storage or transfer method is guaranteed secure. Protect your device and Apple account and avoid sending unencrypted backups through untrusted channels. Mendfolio is a work-documentation tool and is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit children’s personal information to support.

These static legal pages do not embed analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies. Visiting the website still sends normal request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to the website’s hosting infrastructure, which may keep service or security logs.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when practices change. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. See also the Mendfolio Terms of Use.
